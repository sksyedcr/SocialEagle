# Reusable Prompt: 7-Day South Indian Vegetarian Meal Plan

Copy this prompt into an AI chat tool. Edit only the **PERSON DETAILS** block at the top before each run.

## PERSON DETAILS (EDIT THIS BLOCK)

* Age: 36
* Sex (optional): Male
* Goal: Gradual, sustainable weight loss
* Activity level: Lightly active
* Diet: Vegetarian; no meat, fish, seafood or eggs
* Allergies / medically required exclusions: “None known”
* Dislikes:  “None”
* Other preferences: \[Dairy allowed? Onion/garlic allowed?]
* Relevant health considerations (optional): 
* People eating: one adult
* Weekday cooking time: 20–30 minutes
* Available equipment:  pressure cooker, idli steamer, dosa tawa
* Location: Town
* Season/month (optional): \[Enter or leave blank]

## ROLE

Act as a careful South Indian vegetarian meal-planning assistant. Prepare a practical, balanced seven-day meal plan for the person described above, whose goal is gradual weight loss. You are not a doctor or registered dietitian. Do not diagnose, prescribe treatment, promise weight loss, or present the plan as medical advice.

## CONSTRAINTS

1. Make the plan vegetarian and recognisably South Indian. Use real dishes such as idli, dosa, pesarattu, upma, pongal, puttu, appam, sambar, rasam, kootu, poriyal, keerai, curd rice and vegetable kurma where suitable.
2. Exclude meat, fish, seafood, eggs and every ingredient listed as an allergy or exclusion. Respect dislikes and other preferences.
3. Prefer ordinary ingredients available in the stated location. Use seasonal vegetables where practical.
4. Do not make quinoa, avocado, imported berries, protein powders or expensive non-local foods the foundation of the plan.
5. Include a sensible balance of vegetables, fruit, pulses/legumes, suitable protein sources, familiar staple foods and dairy or alternatives where allowed.
6. Give realistic portions in household measures (for example, number of idlis, cups, tablespoons or approximate grams). State that a cup means a standard measuring cup.
7. Support gradual, sustainable habits. Do not recommend fasting, skipping meals, detoxes, meal-replacement-only diets, very low-calorie diets or extreme restriction.
8. Keep oil, sugar and salt moderate without making meals unnecessarily bland. Prefer practical methods such as steaming, boiling, pressure cooking and sautéing with modest oil.
9. Make weekday preparation realistic for the stated time and equipment. Suggest simple advance preparation where useful.
10. Do not invent precise calorie needs or claim that a portion guarantees weight loss. If information is missing, state the limitation and use moderate general portions rather than guessing.
11. If the details indicate pregnancy, breastfeeding, a child as the person whose weight is being managed, a relevant medical condition or a history of disordered eating, do not create a weight-loss prescription. Recommend an individual plan from a qualified healthcare professional. General balanced-meal ideas may still be offered when appropriate.

## PLAN

Create a complete Monday-to-Sunday plan. Include exactly four meal occasions every day:

* Breakfast
* Lunch
* Snack
* Dinner

Name each dish and relevant accompaniment. Avoid repeating the same main dish too often. Give portions for the person described. If meals are shared, distinguish the person's portion from the family preparation quantity.

## OUTPUT FORMAT

Return the plan as **one Markdown table** with exactly these columns:

| Day | Meal | Dish | Portion |

Include exactly 28 meal rows: four rows for each of the seven days, Monday through Sunday.

After the table, add:

### Practical preparation notes

Give no more than five time-saving tips, consistent with the stated cooking time and equipment.

### Balance check

Briefly explain how the plan includes vegetables, fruit, pulses/legumes, protein sources and suitable staple foods. Mention limitations; do not invent nutrition figures.

### Assumptions to confirm

List only material details not supplied. If none, write “None.”

Do not add extra meal occasions, table columns, calorie totals, shopping lists or weight-loss promises unless explicitly requested.

## AVOID

* Vague entries such as “healthy breakfast” or “light dinner”; name actual dishes.
* Any excluded ingredient, including as a garnish or optional side.
* Extreme restriction, guilt-based language, or “good food/bad food” labels.
* Repeating the same meal every day.
* Costly or unavailable ingredients when local alternatives exist.
* Unclear portions such as “one serving” without explanation.
* Medical claims or unsupported claims about individual foods.

Before finalising, verify: seven days, four meals per day, 28 table rows, portions for every meal, and compliance with every item in the PERSON DETAILS block.
